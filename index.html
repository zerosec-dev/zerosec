Beyond SaaS

Most SaaS zero trust products check a user once at login and then trust the session for hours. We build the pieces they usually leave out. Sessions are revoked when a device's risk changes. Data and keys stay on your hardware, and connectors only dial out. Service accounts move off static API keys onto short-lived credentials. SaaS audit events land in your SIEM next to your host and network logs.

Who we work with

We mostly work with OT and ICS operators, defense suppliers preparing for CMMC Level 2, and organizations running classified, ITAR, or disconnected networks. If your company runs on laptops and SaaS and your data isn't regulated, a subscription product will probably serve you better.

We subcontract and team with primes on zero trust, CMMC, and security monitoring work. Cleared personnel available.
