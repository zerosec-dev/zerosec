Zero trust architecture for constrained environments.

Zero trust is an authorization decision your systems make on every request, from the user's claims and the device's current state. It is not a product. Outsource it and the risk stays where it was. Only the understanding moved.

OT floors, classified enclaves, disconnected sites, small DIB shops. These environments cannot reach a cloud policy engine, cannot permit a third party in the decision path, and cannot tolerate auth that fails when the uplink drops. No commercial zero trust product handles that. The constraint is architectural.

I design and build zero trust architectures for these environments. Open standards, deployed on your hardware, policy in plaintext your engineers can read and restore. The engagement ends when your team can trace a denied request to the policy rule that rejected it, explain why, and restore the control plane from runbooks alone.
